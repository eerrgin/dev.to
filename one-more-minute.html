Beyond the calculator

Time isn't the only thing being spent.

The point isn't that scrolling is bad or productivity is sacred. Rest matters. Play matters. So does choosing, instead of drifting into a choice. The same number can mean escape, connection, curiosity, or avoidance.

01 / NOTICE

Did you choose it, or did it choose you?

Some habits begin as decisions and become the default setting. Noticing the difference is already a form of agency.

02 / VALUE

What would make the time feel well spent?

There is no universal answer. A quiet hour can restore you; a busy hour can empty you. The label matters less than the experience.

03 / EXPERIMENT

What happens if you change just ten minutes?

You don't need a grand reinvention. Try a tiny change for a week, pay attention, and keep what genuinely helps.

A question to take with you

If your time could leave a review, what would it say about today?

No score. No streak. No account to create. Just a moment to decide what you want your attention to serve next.
